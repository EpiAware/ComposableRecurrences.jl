Depletion forms ​

A new depletion form sets how a pool gives up what a step asks for, building on Writing new types. Depletion forms lists the built-in forms.

A form with a pullback ​

A depletion form draws value v from pool s with population N. Its parameters, such as the exponent of Hazard, are its fields. It is pointwise: it draws each entry of v from the same entry of s. Passed to Depletion as a draw, it takes from the first pool.

where is the value drawn and the pool after the step. Its pullback overwrites grads.v and grads.s with the cotangents of v and s. It reads no N, so it adds nothing into grads.N; Depletion adds what a form puts there into its own mirror. grads.piece is the mirror of the form's own fields and takes their cotangents.

julia
struct Capped end
function CR.forward!(::Capped, ::CR.Step, v, s, N)
    for k in eachindex(v, s)
        y = min(v[k], s[k])
        v[k], s[k] = y, s[k] - y
    end
    return nothing
end
function CR.pullback!(grads, ::Capped, ::CR.Step, v, s, N)
    v̄, s̄ = grads.v, grads.s
    for k in eachindex(v, s)
        if v[k] <= s[k]
            v̄[k] -= s̄[k]
        else
            v̄[k], s̄[k] = zero(v̄[k]), v̄[k]
        end
    end
    return nothing
end
