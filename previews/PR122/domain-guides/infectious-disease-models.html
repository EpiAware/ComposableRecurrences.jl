Infectious disease models ​

Many infectious disease models are recurrences and convolutions. This table maps common model parts to the code that builds them, with the tutorial that uses each. is infections, the reproduction number and the generation interval on lag .

Model partMathsCodeTutorial
Renewal process Recurrence(w), called as r(R; history)Renewal then delay
Susceptible depletion  ,   Depletion(N), or Depletion(N, Floor())Renewal then delay
All-or-nothing vaccination, efficacy , doses a share of doses leaves :    Depletion(N; removals = TimeVarying(e .* d), protected = Protected(0))Renewal then delay
Leaky vaccination, efficacy , doses infections from   , doses move from to Depletion(N; removals = TimeVarying(d), protected = Protected(1 - e))Renewal then delay
Imported cases   add = ι, or Add(TimeVarying(ι)) after depletionRenewal then delay
Reporting delay  Convolution(d)Renewal then delay
Log reproduction number as a latent process  Recurrence([ρ])(; history, add = ϵ)Latent processes driving R_t
Delay that changes over time  or  Convolution(TimeVarying(D)), or TimeVarying(D, Primary())Time-varying delays and kernels
Mixing between places or groups Recurrence(w; coupling = M)Spatial and multi-type models
Generation interval per group or pair or PerStratum(W), Pairwise(A)Spatial and multi-type models
Importation between patchesa share of patch 's infections occurs in patch Redistribute(K, ε)Spatial and multi-type models
Districts sharing a patch total fixed elsewhere  , with district 's own renewal valueAllocate(groups, TimeVarying(PerStratum(Ī)))Spatial and multi-type models
Isolation   a thinned kernel w .* (1 .- p * b .* F)Spatial and multi-type models
Isolation that starts on a set day, by infection day  ,        Recurrence(TimeVarying(W, Primary())) with seededTime-varying delays and kernels
Branching-process extinction by generation ,  , with the offspring probability generating functionRecurrence([1.0]; modifiers = (Transform(G, θ),)), called as r(; history = [0.0], stop)Transforms inside a recurrence
Behavioural response to incidence  Transform((v, κ) -> v / (1 + v / κ), κ), before DepletionTransforms inside a recurrence
Bed occupancy   Recurrence([1 - δ])(; history, add = A), or Convolution((1 - δ) .^ (0:T-1))Occupancy and capacity
Bed cap Clamp(0, B)Occupancy and capacity
Forecast after a fitcontinue from the fitted dayswith_state, then r(R; state)Renewal then delay
Seed days returned with the run then the renewalseeded(r, R; history)Renewal then delay
