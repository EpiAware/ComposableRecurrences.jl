ComposableRecurrences ​

Fast, composable and differentiable recurrences and convolutions in Julia.

Why ComposableRecurrences? ​

  • A recurrence computes each value from its own past values, such as infections from earlier infections. A convolution weights the past values of another series, such as reported cases from earlier infections.

  • Both are usually hand-written loops, rewritten for each model. Recurrence and Convolution express them as two operators, for one series or for many strata, such as towns, run side by side.

  • Coupling between strata, finite pools and bounds are added by composing small couplings and modifiers.

  • The forward run and gradients under ForwardDiff, Mooncake and Enzyme are close to or faster than a hand-written loop; see the performance figure.

Getting started ​

Three towns share an outbreak. Infections in each town come from earlier infections in that town and, through travel, in the other towns. Each town has a finite number of people who can be infected. A share of infections is reported after a delay. An intervention from day 50 lowers the reproduction number in every town.

The value is the number of new infections before depletion, and the susceptible pool starts at the population . The generation interval has weight on lag over lags, the reporting delay has weight over lags, and a share   of infections is reported. julia
using ComposableRecurrences
using ComposableRecurrences: Depletion

gi = [0.05, 0.2, 0.3, 0.25, 0.12, 0.08]
T = 75
towns = ["A", "B", "C"]
pop = [60_000.0, 25_000.0, 10_000.0]
dist = [0.0 20.0 45.0; 20.0 0.0 30.0; 45.0 30.0 0.0]
gravity = [a == b ? 0.0 : pop[b] / dist[a, b]^2 for a in 1:3, b in 1:3]
K = 0.998 * [a == b for a in 1:3, b in 1:3] + 0.002 * gravity ./ sum(gravity; dims = 2)
seed = [fill(10.0, 1, 6); zeros(2, 6)]
R = [1.8 - clamp((t - 50) / 6, 0, 1) for _ in towns, t in 1:T]

renewal = Recurrence(gi; coupling = K, modifiers = (Depletion(PerStratum(pop)),))
delay = Convolution([0.0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.3, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05])
infections = renewal(R; history = seed)
reports = delay(infections; gain = 0.4)

Related packages ​

  • ComposableTuringIDModels.jl builds infectious disease models from renewal, delay and latent process components.

  • ConvolvedDistributions.jl builds convolutions of distributions, such as the total of two independent delays.

  • CensoredDistributions.jl discretises delay distributions into the probability mass functions these operators take as kernels.
